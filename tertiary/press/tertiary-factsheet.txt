TAGLINE
The hero gets the story. You get the upgrades.

ONE LINE
You're the NPC by the well in someone else's RPG: buy upgrades for yourself
and the game around you until the Hero finally notices you.

SHORT DESCRIPTION
An active comedy incremental about the NPC by the well. Earn Screen Time every
time the Hero walks past, then upgrade yourself and the game you're stuck in:
a second line of dialogue, legs (beta), 16-bit color, a kazoo, a chiptune
band. The hero gets the story. You get the upgrades.

LONG DESCRIPTION
You are Villager #3. You stand by the well in Mossbrook and say one line:
"Welcome to Mossbrook!" You have four colors (all green), no legs, no music
and no name. Every 45 seconds or so, Lance Valorheart, the Hero, walks past on
his way to the real adventure. He has never once looked at you.

Every time he walks past, you earn Screen Time. Spend it: on a second line of
dialogue, Legs (beta), a name up to 8 letters long, a wooden spoon, 16-bit
color that spreads out from you while the Hero stays stubbornly monochrome,
and a soundtrack that starts as one kazoo.

Say your line at exactly the right moment, photobomb his cutscenes, beat
Villager #2 to the Hero's errands, and catch Gerald, Chicken #3, the only one
who has always noticed you. Every purchase changes how Mossbrook looks, sounds
or plays, and the whole town has opinions about it.

FEATURES
- An active comedy incremental where nearly every upgrade visibly or audibly
  changes the game, with a joke attached
- 16-bit JRPG pixel art that starts in four shades of Game Boy green and earns
  every color
- An original chiptune soundtrack that grows from silence to kazoo to band to
  16-bit orchestra. No licensed music, so it's safe to stream and monetize
- Active without frantic clicking: hold to wave, a Relaxed or Auto assist for
  the timing mini-games, and some progress while you're away
- Every reveal can be skipped after half a second and replayed in the Upgrade
  Museum
- Mouse, keyboard or controller
- Three acts, about 4–6 hours to the credits, then New Game+
