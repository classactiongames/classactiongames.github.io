TAGLINE
The hero gets the story. You get the upgrades.

ONE LINE
You're the NPC by the well in someone else's RPG, buying upgrades for yourself
and the game around you until the Hero finally notices you.

SHORT DESCRIPTION
An active comedy incremental about the NPC by the well. Every time the Hero
walks past you earn Screen Time to spend on yourself and on the game you're
stuck in, from a second line of dialogue and Legs (beta) to 16-bit color and a
kazoo. The hero gets the story. You get the upgrades.

LONG DESCRIPTION
You are Villager #3. You stand by the well in Mossbrook and say one line,
"Welcome to Mossbrook!", to whoever walks past. You have no legs and no name,
and all four of your colors are green. There isn't any music, either. Lance
Valorheart, the Hero, keeps passing through on his way to the real adventure.
He has never once looked at you.

Every time he walks past you earn Screen Time. It pays for a second line of
dialogue, for Legs (beta) and for a name of up to 8 letters, and later for
16-bit color that spreads out from you while the Hero stays stubbornly
monochrome. Even the soundtrack has to be bought, starting with one kazoo.

Getting noticed is mostly timing. You say your line at exactly the right
moment as he passes, you photobomb his cutscenes, and once you have legs you
race Villager #2 to the Hero's errands. Gerald, Chicken #3, is the only one
who has always noticed you, if you can catch him. The whole town has opinions
about every purchase, since each one changes something you can see or hear.

FEATURES
- Nearly every upgrade changes something you can see or hear, usually with a
  joke attached.
- 16-bit JRPG pixel art that starts out in four shades of Game Boy green and
  has to earn every other color.
- An original chiptune soundtrack that grows from silence, by way of a kazoo,
  into a 16-bit orchestra. It uses no licensed music, so it's safe to stream and
  monetize.
- It's active without being frantic. Waving means holding a button, not
  hammering it, while the timing mini-games come with Relaxed and Auto assists.
  Some progress even carries on while you're away.
- Any reveal can be skipped after half a second and watched again later in the
  Upgrade Museum.
- Plays with mouse and keyboard or a controller.
- Three acts, with New Game+ after the credits.
