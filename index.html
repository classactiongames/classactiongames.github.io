Statement of the name

Three jokes, one name

In case you were about to ask: no, we can't help with your lawsuit. The name reads three ways.

  1. Count I

    Class

    As in an RPG class: the thing you pick before the adventure starts. Fighter, mage, villager by the well.

  2. Count II

    Class action

    As in a lawsuit filed on behalf of a lot of annoyed people at once. Here, players against boredom.

  3. Count III

    Action games

    As in games. We make them. That part is not a joke.

Class Action Games is a game studio, not a law firm. Nothing on this site is legal advice. Grievances go to the Discord.
