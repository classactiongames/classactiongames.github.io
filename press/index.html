Exhibit L

Studio logos

SVG for anything that scales, PNG with a transparent background otherwise. Every variant is in the ZIP.

Please: leave clear space around the logo (the height of the capital letters on every side of a lockup; an eighth of the width around the seal), use the seal at 128 px and up and the solid round mark below that, and keep to the colours as given. Don't stretch, recolour, tilt or add effects to the logos, and don't retype the wordmark.
